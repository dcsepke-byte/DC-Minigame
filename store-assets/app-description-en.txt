## Description (max 4000 chars)

🎉 **PARTY ARENA** — The party game for you and your friends!

Compete in 14+ fast-paced minigames, roll the dice across the 3D game board, and collect stars to be crowned champion. One screen is all you need — the host displays the game on a big screen, everyone else plays on their phone.

**HOW IT WORKS:**
📱 One device is the host (tablet/laptop/screen)
📲 Everyone else joins via room code on their phone
🎮 Choose from 14+ minigames or start Board Mode
🏆 Collect stars, level up, and unlock new characters

**GAME MODES:**
• **Classic Mix** — Round by round minigames, earn points and stars
• **Board Party** — Roll dice, buy tiles, duel opponents — like a digital board game
• **Solo Challenge** — Practice alone and beat your high scores

**MINIGAMES (14+):**
• Tower Stack — Stack blocks with perfect timing
• Ninja Slash — Fruit Ninja style: slice fruit, dodge bombs
• Dodgeball — Dodge incoming balls, getting faster!
• Bubble Pop — Tap only your color, trigger chain reactions
• Color Catch — Catch the right colors, avoid wrong ones
• Rhythm Tap — Tap to the beat, build combos
• Quick Draw Duel — Western duel: who taps faster?
• Coin Dash — Collect coins, avoid enemies
• Tile Flip — Memory puzzle with boosters
• Bounce Survival — Keep the ball alive with your paddle
• And many more!

**FEATURES:**
✨ XP & Level System — get better, level up
⭐ Star Currency — unlock 8+ characters and trails
🏅 10 Achievements — master all challenges
📅 Daily Challenge — bonus task every day
🌍 German & English
📳 Haptic Feedback (vibration)
🎵 Original soundtrack & sound effects

**PERFECT FOR:**
• Game nights with friends
• Family game time
• Parties & events
• On the go — one phone per player is all you need

**NO ADS, NO TRACKING.**
Party Arena respects your privacy. No analytics tools, no ad networks. Your game progress stays on your device.

Download Party Arena now and become the champion of the arena!
